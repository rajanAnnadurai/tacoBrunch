The Plan Menu Helpers
For the host

Shopping List

For 15 people · Sunday morning prep

Produce

  • 4 large limes (zest + juice across recipes)
  • 2 lb yellow onions (~3 medium)
  • 1 lb red onions (~2 medium, for pickling + pico)
  • 4 large poblano peppers
  • 1 jalapeño
  • 1.5 lb roma tomatoes (~6, for pico)
  • 2 large bunches cilantro
  • 1 head iceberg or romaine (shredded lettuce)
  • 1 small head red cabbage (slaw for prawns)
  • 1.5 lb cremini or baby bella mushrooms
  • ¾ lb yukon gold potatoes (for egg tacos)
  • 2 garlic heads (~12 cloves used)
  • 3 ripe avocados (for guacamole)
  • 1 small watermelon (~4 lb, for agua fresca)
  • 1 lb strawberries
  • 1 pint blueberries
  • 2 ears corn OR 1 lb frozen corn (esquites)
  • 1 pink grapefruit OR 6-pack grapefruit soda (palomas)

Meat / Seafood

  • 2 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • 1 lb large peeled, deveined shrimp (16/20 count) — about 24 prawns

Dairy / Eggs

  • 1 dozen eggs
  • ½ lb shredded Monterey Jack or Oaxaca cheese
  • 8 oz queso fresco
  • 16 oz sour cream or Mexican crema
  • 1 stick butter

Pantry

  • Achiote paste (3 oz pack — for chicken marinade)
  • Chipotles in adobo (small can — for prawns)
  • 2 cans (15 oz) black beans
  • 2 lb long-grain white rice
  • Olive oil
  • Kosher salt, black pepper
  • Smoked paprika, cumin, dried oregano
  • White vinegar (for pickled onions)
  • 2 large bags tortilla chips
  • 2 jars salsa (one mild, one hot) OR roja + verde
  • 1 jar salsa verde for the toppings bar
  • Hot sauce (2-3 bottles, variety)
  • Cotija cheese (for esquites)

Tortillas

  • 30 corn tortillas (small)
  • 14 flour tortillas (small/medium — for egg tacos)

Drinks / Bar

  • 1 bottle blanco tequila (750ml or 1L)
  • 1 bottle triple sec / orange liqueur
  • 1 large bottle margarita mix OR fresh lime + simple syrup
  • 4-pack grapefruit soda (Squirt or Jarritos) for palomas
  • 1 case Mexican lager (Modelo, Pacifico, or similar)
  • Coarse salt for margarita rims
  • Extra ice (2 bags)

Bakery / Bought

  • Tres leches cake — serves 15 (call the bakery in advance)

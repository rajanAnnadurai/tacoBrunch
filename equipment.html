For the host · gather Saturday night

What you'll need

Cookware, bowls, and tools — split between the outdoor grill and indoor stove.

How the kitchen splits

The outdoor grill handles all the high-heat cooking: chicken on the grates, prawns + tortillas on a cast-iron griddle, mushrooms + esquites in two skillets that ride directly on the grates. The indoor stove stays light: just rice, beans, eggs, and warming flour tortillas — no more than two burners going at once.

Cookware by location

Outdoor

Grill + 2 skillets

  • Outdoor grill (gas or charcoal — propane checked / charcoal ready)
  • Cast iron griddle (the existing one — for prawns, then tortillas)
  • Large skillet #1 — for mushrooms & esquites
  • Large skillet #2 — second mushroom batch in parallel
  • Long grill tongs
  • Grill spatula
  • Grill brush (clean before lighting)
  • Lighter / matches
Indoor

Stovetop cookware

  • 12-inch nonstick skillet — eggs (medium-low needs precise heat)
  • Cast iron skillet (smaller) — crispy potatoes for egg tacos
  • Saucepan, medium — black beans
  • 3-quart pot with lid — rice (must stay covered, low heat, 18 min)
  • Comal or dry pan — warming the 22 flour tortillas
  • Sheet pan / cookie tray — optional, for cooling, transferring, drying

Bowls + serving

Mixing bowls

  • 2 large mixing bowls (chicken marinade, eggs)
  • 3-4 medium bowls (shrimp marinade, slaw, pico, guac, esquites — can rotate as you finish)
  • 1 large bowl with plate or wrap (steaming charred poblanos)

Serving

  • 4 serving bowls (mushroom filling, rice, beans, esquites)
  • 10 small bowls (toppings bar — pico · guac · queso fresco · lettuce · sour cream · lemon wedges · cilantro · pickled onions · salsa verde · hot sauce)
  • 1 warm platter (sliced chicken)
  • 1 platter (prawns + slaw side)
  • Tortilla warmer OR 2 clean kitchen towels

Drinks + the bar

Pitchers + jars

  • 2 large pitchers (≥ 2 quarts each) — margaritas (2 batches)
  • 1 large pitcher or jug (~9 cups) — watermelon agua fresca
  • Mason jar or glass container — pickled red onions
  • Cooler — beer + ice

Drinkware

  • 15-20 glasses — margaritas, palomas, water, agua fresca (sturdy stemless or cups)
  • Ice scoop or extra tongs
  • Lemon wedge plate at the bar
  • Small dish of coarse salt for margarita rims

Knives + tools

Cutting

  • 2-3 sharp chef's knives (multiple stations need them at once)
  • 2-3 cutting boards (separate raw protein from produce)
  • Paring knife (avocado, jalapeño)

Hand utensils

  • 2 whisks
  • 2-3 wooden spoons or silicone spatulas
  • Soft egg spatula (rubber/silicone — gentle on eggs)
  • Indoor tongs (separate from grill tongs)
  • Citrus juicer or reamer (you'll juice ~20 lemons from Rajan's tree — a press saves your hands)
  • Fork (rice fluffing, guac mashing)
  • 10 small spoons (one per topping bowl)
  • Instant-read thermometer (chicken doneness — 165°F)
  • Ladle or large spoon (margarita / agua fresca pouring)

Small appliances + service

Appliances

  • Blender (watermelon agua fresca)
  • Fine-mesh sieve / strainer (optional, for smoother agua fresca)

Service ware

  • 15 plates (real or compostable)
  • 15-20 forks
  • ~30 napkins (cloth or paper)
  • Plastic wrap (marinade covers, guac surface, poblano steaming)
  • Aluminum foil (tenting chicken to rest)
  • Paper towels
  • 2 bags of ice

Station 3 · Prawns

Chipotle-Garlic Prawns

Cook: ____________ · Active 12:10 – 12:25 · Last protein on, fastest to cook

⏱ Your Timeline

  1. 11:30 — Make the cabbage slaw, refrigerate
  2. 11:45 — Make the chipotle marinade, mix with shrimp, leave 15-20 min
  3. 12:10 — Heat cast iron griddle (or skillet) screaming hot
  4. 12:15 — Sear shrimp 2 min per side
  5. 12:25 — Plate with slaw on the side

Ingredients

Makes 20 tacos (2 prawns per taco).

Shrimp

  • 2 lb large peeled, deveined shrimp (16/20 count) — about 40 prawns
  • 2-3 chipotles in adobo (from a small can), finely minced + 2 tbsp adobo sauce
  • 6 garlic cloves, minced
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • Juice of 2 limes
  • 1 ½ tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 ½ tsp kosher salt

Cabbage-lime slaw

  • ¾ small head red cabbage, finely shredded (~6 cups)
  • Juice of 1 ½ limes
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • ¾ tsp salt
  • Handful chopped cilantro

Equipment

  • Cast iron griddle or heavy skillet — see griddle handoff note below
  • Two mixing bowls
  • Tongs
  • Sharp knife + cutting board

Heads up — griddle handoff: the outdoor cast iron griddle gets handed off to Bar/Toppings cook at 12:25 to warm corn tortillas. Use it 12:10–12:25, then move off. (Or use an indoor heavy skillet for prawns and let Bar take the outdoor griddle from 12:15.)

Steps

1

Make the slaw (11:30)

Toss shredded cabbage, lime juice, olive oil, salt, and cilantro in a bowl. Refrigerate. The acid softens the cabbage so it's not crunchy-stiff.

2

Marinade the shrimp (11:45)

In a bowl, mix minced chipotle + adobo + garlic + olive oil + lime juice + smoked paprika + salt. Add shrimp, toss to coat. Set aside 15-20 minutes — not longer (the lime starts to "cook" the shrimp).

3

Sear (12:15)

Heat the cast iron over high heat for 3-4 minutes — it should be seriously hot. A drop of water should evaporate instantly.

40 prawns won't fit at once. Plan for 2-3 batches (about 15-20 per batch in single layer). Each batch takes ~4 minutes total. Crowding steams instead of sears.

Lay the first batch in a single layer. Don't touch them for 2 minutes. They'll release naturally when ready. Flip, another 1.5-2 minutes. Move to a platter; repeat.

4

Watch the shape — your foolproof timer

Shrimp tells you when it's done. Raw shrimp is straight. "C" shape = done. "O" shape (tightly curled) = overcooked, will be rubbery. Pull them at the C, every time. They keep cooking on the plate from residual heat.

5

Plate

Pile shrimp on a platter, slaw next to (not on top of — stays crisp). Bring straight to the bar — shrimp don't reheat well.

🚨 Help! Something's wrong

  • Shrimp are tough/rubbery. Overcooked. Next batch: pull at the "C" not the "O". 90 seconds per side is plenty.
  • Marinated too long. If they look opaque before cooking (the lime "cooked" them), still grill briefly — they'll be fine.
  • Sticking to the griddle. Pan wasn't hot enough. Don't force the flip — wait 30 more seconds.

✅ Final checklist

  • Slaw made and chilled
  • Shrimp pulled at "C" shape (not over)
  • Plated and brought out immediately

Station 2 · Grilled Chicken

Achiote Grilled Chicken

Cook: ____________ · Marinate 10:30 · Grill 12:00 · Slice 12:20

⏱ Your Timeline

  1. 10:30 — Mix marinade in a big bowl
  2. 10:40 — Coat chicken thighs, refrigerate (75 min in marinade)
  3. 11:30 — Light the grill, get it to medium-high
  4. 12:00 — Grill chicken: ~6 min per side
  5. 12:15 — Pull off, rest 5 min, slice against the grain
  6. 12:25 — Plate on a warm platter, bring to the bar

Ingredients

Makes 20 tacos.

  • 2 ½ lb boneless skinless chicken thighs (≈ 10 thighs)
  • 4 tbsp achiote (annatto) paste
  • Juice of 3 limes (~⅓ cup)
  • 5 garlic cloves, minced or grated
  • ⅓ cup olive oil
  • 1 ½ tsp dried oregano
  • ¾ tsp ground cumin
  • 1 ½ tsp kosher salt
  • ¾ tsp black pepper

Equipment

  • Outdoor grill
  • Large mixing bowl + whisk
  • Tongs
  • Cutting board + sharp knife
  • Warm platter for sliced chicken
  • Instant-read thermometer (recommended)

Steps

1

Mix the marinade

In a big bowl, whisk together: achiote paste, lime juice, garlic, olive oil, oregano, cumin, salt, pepper. Mix until the paste fully dissolves into a smooth red-orange sauce. It will look like a thick vinaigrette.

2

Coat the chicken

Pat thighs dry with paper towels (this helps the marinade stick). Add chicken to the bowl, toss with your hands or tongs until every piece is coated. Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate. Aim for ≥ 60 minutes; longer is fine up to 4 hours.

3

Grill

Light the grill at 11:30. Grill should be medium-high — you can hold your hand 5 inches above the grate for about 3 seconds before pulling away.

At 12:00, lay thighs on the grill, smooth side down. Don't move them for 6 minutes — let them get good grill marks. Flip with tongs, grill another 5-6 minutes.

Done temp: 165°F internal (or juices run clear when pierced at the thickest part).

4

Rest

Move chicken to a cutting board. Tent loosely with foil. Walk away for 5 minutes — this is critical. If you cut now, all the juice runs out and the chicken dries out.

5

Slice and plate

Slice each thigh across the grain (the lines of the meat) into ½-inch strips. Pile on a warm platter. Drizzle any board juices back over the meat. Bring to the bar.

🚨 Help! Something's wrong

  • Sticking to the grate. Don't try to move it yet. Wait another minute and it will release naturally when ready.
  • Outside burning before inside cooks. Move chicken to a cooler part of the grill (or close the lid with vents partially open) to finish slower.
  • Chicken looks dry. Slice a thicker piece in half — pink near the bone is bad, but a hint of pink throughout cooked meat is fine. Use the thermometer (165°F).
  • No achiote paste available. Substitute: 2 tbsp paprika + 1 tbsp tomato paste + ½ tsp turmeric. Same color, similar earthy flavor.

✅ Final checklist

  • Chicken hit 165°F internal
  • Rested 5 minutes minimum
  • Sliced against the grain
  • Plated on warm platter, juices over the top

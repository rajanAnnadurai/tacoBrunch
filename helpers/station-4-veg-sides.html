1️⃣ Mushroom + Poblano Filling

Makes 20 tacos. (Note: this is the only vegetarian option — 4 of your guests rely on this. Watch the platter and refill or pull cooks early if it's running low.)

  • 2½ lb cremini mushrooms, sliced (mushrooms cook down ~50% — don't cut this back)
  • 4 large poblano peppers (charred + peeled, then sliced)
  • 1 large yellow onion, sliced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 ½ tsp ground cumin
  • 1 ½ tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 ½ tsp salt
A

Char the poblanos (10:45)

Lay whole poblanos on the hot grill. Turn every 2 min until skin is blistered black on all sides (~8-10 min). Move to a bowl, cover with plastic wrap or a plate. Steam 10 min — this loosens the skin. Then peel the blackened skin off (it slips off), remove stem and seeds, slice into strips.

B

Sauté (11:30) — Outdoor 2 skillets in parallel

2½ lb of sliced mushrooms won't fit one pan single-layer. The grill has space for two skillets at once — use both, batches happen simultaneously instead of back-to-back. Total time ~15 min instead of ~25.

Both skillets at once: Heat 1½ tbsp oil in each skillet on the grill (medium-high). Split the onion between the two pans — sauté 2 min. Split the mushrooms between the two pans — don't stir for 4 min (let them brown). Toss, sauté another 3 min in each pan.

Combine + finish (12:00): Move everything from skillet 2 into skillet 1. Add all the poblano strips, garlic, cumin, paprika, and salt. Cook 3 more min on the grill (or back to medium-high indoor if you prefer) to combine and warm through. Taste, adjust salt. Move to a serving bowl.

2️⃣ Cilantro-Lime Rice

  • 2 cups long-grain white rice
  • 3 cups water + 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp olive oil or butter
  • Juice of 1½ limes
  • ½ cup chopped cilantro
C

Cook (11:00) — Indoor stove

Rinse rice in cold water until water runs almost clear (helps fluffiness). Combine rice + water + salt + oil in a pot. Bring to a boil, cover, reduce to lowest heat. Cook 18 minutes — don't lift the lid. After 18 min, kill heat, leave covered another 5 min. Then fluff with a fork, stir in lime juice and cilantro.

3️⃣ Doctored Black Beans

  • 3 cans (15 oz) black beans (do not drain)
  • ½ medium onion, finely diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • ½ tsp salt + pepper
D

Simmer (11:45) — Indoor stove

Heat olive oil in a saucepan over medium. Sauté onion 3 min, add garlic and cumin, 1 more min. Add beans (with their liquid). Simmer 12-15 min, stirring occasionally. Smash a few beans with a spoon to thicken. Salt and pepper to taste.

4️⃣ Esquites (Mexican Street Corn Salad)

Makes ~15 standard side servings (⅓ cup each). Served salad-style in small bowls or cups, scooped with a spoon — not on the cob.

  • 5 cups corn kernels (from 6 ears fresh, or 1½ lb frozen, thawed)
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • ½ cup mayo
  • ½ cup crumbled cotija (sub feta if needed)
  • Juice of 1½ limes
  • ¼ cup chopped cilantro
  • ¾ tsp chili powder + ¾ tsp smoked paprika
  • ½ tsp salt
E

Char and dress (11:15) — Outdoor skillet on grill

Heat 1½ tbsp butter in a skillet on the grill over high heat. Add half the corn in a single layer (don't crowd — that steams instead of chars). Don't stir for 3-4 min — let kernels char. Toss, char another 2 min. Move to a bowl. Repeat with the second half + remaining butter. Cool 2 min.

Stir in mayo, cotija, lime juice, cilantro, chili powder, paprika, salt. Taste — adjust salt or lime as needed.

🚨 Help! Something's wrong

  • Rice is mushy. Too much water or lifted the lid. Spread on a sheet pan to cool/dry briefly, then re-fluff.
  • Mushrooms went soggy. Pan crowded or not hot enough. Drain off liquid, crank heat, char what's left.
  • Beans too dry. Add ¼ cup water or chicken stock, simmer 2 min.
  • Esquites too tangy. Add a pinch of sugar or extra cotija.

✅ Final checklist

  • Mushroom + poblano filling, hot, in serving bowl
  • Rice fluffed and dressed
  • Beans simmered and seasoned
  • Esquites in a bowl, cotija on top

Station 1 · Egg Tacos

Austin Breakfast Tacos

Cook: ____________ · Active 11:45 – 12:30 · Cooked fresh as guests arrive

⏱ Your Timeline

  1. 11:45 — Wash and dice potatoes, start them frying
  2. 12:00 — Crack and whisk eggs (don't cook yet)
  3. 12:15 — Set out flour tortillas, warm cheese
  4. 12:25 — Cook eggs in batches (~7 per batch, 3 batches)
  5. 12:30 — Plate and bring to the bar; cook fresh batches as needed

Ingredients

Makes 20 tacos.

  • 20 eggs (≈ 1 ½ dozen + 2)
  • 1 ¼ lb yukon gold potatoes, ¼-inch dice
  • 1 ½ cups shredded Monterey Jack or Oaxaca cheese
  • 22 small flour tortillas
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • Kosher salt, black pepper
  • Optional: 1 tsp smoked paprika for the potatoes

Equipment

  • One large nonstick skillet (12-inch) for eggs
  • One cast iron skillet or heavy pan for potatoes
  • Whisk, large bowl
  • Plates or warm tray for finished food

Steps

1

Crisp the potatoes

Heat 2 tbsp olive oil in the cast iron pan over medium-high. When it shimmers, add the diced potatoes in a single layer. Don't crowd — work in two batches if needed. Sprinkle with salt and (if using) smoked paprika.

Cook undisturbed for 4 minutes — let them brown. Then toss and cook another 5-7 minutes until golden and crispy. Move to a paper-towel-lined plate.

2

Whisk the eggs

Crack all 20 eggs into a big bowl. Add 1 tsp salt and a few cracks of black pepper. Whisk until uniform yellow, no streaks. Don't cook them yet — wait until 12:25.

3

Cook eggs in batches

Heat 1 tbsp butter in the big nonstick over medium-low (not high — that makes rubbery eggs). Pour in about ⅓ of the egg mixture (~7 eggs). Let it set for 30 seconds, then gently push from the edges to the center with a spatula. Stop while still slightly wet — they'll finish cooking on the plate.

Slide onto a plate, sprinkle with cheese and a handful of crispy potatoes. Repeat for batches 2 and 3 as guests arrive — 3 batches total.

4

Bring it to the bar

Set warm flour tortillas next to the eggs and potatoes. Guests build their own (egg + potato + cheese, then to the toppings bar for salsa).

🚨 Help! Something's wrong

  • Eggs went rubbery. Heat too high or cooked too long. Lower heat next batch and pull off when still slightly wet.
  • Potatoes are soggy. Pan was too crowded or not hot enough. Spread out next batch, more oil, higher heat.
  • Tortillas are stiff. Wrap in a damp towel and microwave 30 sec, or warm on the griddle 15 sec/side.

✅ Final checklist

  • Potatoes cooked and held warm
  • Eggs cooked fresh (not held more than 5 min)
  • Tortillas warmed and wrapped
  • Cheese set out next to eggs

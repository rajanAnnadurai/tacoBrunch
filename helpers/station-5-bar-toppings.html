1️⃣ Pickled Red Onions (10:45)

  • 1 medium red onion, thinly sliced
  • ½ cup white vinegar
  • ½ cup hot water
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp salt

Combine vinegar, water, sugar, salt in a jar. Stir to dissolve. Add onion. Press down so all submerged. Sit for ≥ 30 min — actually gets better the longer it sits.

2️⃣ Pico de Gallo (11:00)

  • 8 roma tomatoes, small dice
  • 1 medium white or yellow onion, fine dice
  • 1 jalapeño, seeded + minced (taste — start with half)
  • ⅔ cup chopped cilantro
  • Juice of 1½ limes
  • 1¼ tsp salt
A

Make it

Combine everything in a bowl, toss. Taste — needs salt? More lime? Adjust. Drain off some liquid before serving (pico shouldn't be soupy).

3️⃣ Watermelon Agua Fresca (11:15)

  • 6 cups cubed watermelon (~ ⅓ small melon)
  • 3 cups cold water
  • Juice of 1½ limes
  • 3 tbsp sugar (or to taste)
  • Pinch of salt
  • Ice for serving

Blend watermelon + water until smooth. Strain through a fine-mesh sieve into a pitcher (optional — leave un-strained for more texture). Stir in lime, sugar, salt. Chill in fridge until serving. Serve over ice.

4️⃣ Margarita Pitchers — make 2 (11:30)

For 15 adults at a celebration, two pitchers is the right starting amount (≈ 16 servings, with leftovers). Make them at the same time.

Per pitcher (8 servings):

  • 1½ cups blanco tequila (× 2 = 3 cups)
  • 1 cup fresh lime juice, ~8 limes (× 2 = 2 cups, ~16 limes)
  • ¾ cup triple sec / orange liqueur (× 2 = 1½ cups)
  • ½ cup simple syrup, or 3 tbsp agave (× 2 = 1 cup, or 6 tbsp agave)
  • Coarse salt + lime wedge for rims
  • Ice

Combine tequila + lime + triple sec + syrup in each pitcher. Stir. Refrigerate. To serve: rub a lime wedge on the rim of each glass, dip in salt, fill with ice, pour.

5️⃣ Palomas (set up at the bar)

Self-serve format. Set out:

  • Bottle of blanco tequila
  • 4-pack grapefruit soda (Squirt, Jarritos)
  • Lime wedges
  • Salt for rims (optional)
  • Glasses + ice

Recipe card to set out: 2 oz tequila + grapefruit soda + squeeze of lime, over ice.

6️⃣ Guacamole (12:00)

  • 6 ripe avocados (use 8 if your crowd loves guac)
  • Juice of 2 limes
  • ½ medium red onion, finely diced
  • ¼ cup chopped cilantro
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Optional: 1 jalapeño minced
B

Make at the last moment

Halve avocados, pop pits out, scoop flesh into a bowl. Squeeze in lime first (prevents browning). Mash with a fork to chunky-creamy — leave some texture. Stir in onion, cilantro, salt, jalapeño. Taste — more lime/salt if needed. Press plastic wrap directly on the surface if not serving immediately.

7️⃣ Tortilla Warming (12:20)

C

Warm 'em right

You're warming 60 corn tortillas (chicken + prawns + mushroom). Flour tortillas (22, for egg tacos) are handled by the Egg station on the indoor stove.

Heat the outdoor cast iron griddle over medium. Lay 6-8 tortillas flat, 30 seconds per side, until soft and slightly puffed. Stack into a tortilla warmer (or wrap in a clean kitchen towel — it holds heat ~15 min). Repeat in batches.

Strategy: warm the first 30 by 12:30 (when the bar opens) and keep cycling 10-15 fresh ones every few minutes as guests come back for seconds. A stack that sits too long gets stiff.

Note on the griddle: the Prawn cook uses it 12:10–12:25. Take over at 12:25 and start your warming.

8️⃣ Toppings Bar Layout (12:25)

D

Plate it pretty

Set up bar from left to right in build order: tortillas → fillings → toppings → drinks.

Toppings in small bowls, each with a spoon: pico, guac, queso fresco, shredded lettuce, sour cream, lime wedges, cilantro, pickled red onions, salsa verde, hot sauce.

Set out 15 plates, napkins, forks at the start of the line.

🚨 Help! Something's wrong

  • Margarita too sweet. Add more lime juice. Too tart? More syrup.
  • Guac browning. Squeeze fresh lime over top, press plastic wrap directly on surface.
  • Pico is watery. Drain off liquid through a strainer before serving.
